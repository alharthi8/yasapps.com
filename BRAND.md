# Yas Apps — Open Wings

Yas Apps has its own parent identity. **Open Wings** combines two lifted geometric wings with a separate folded base. Together they suggest a Y and a family of connected tools. The open gaps keep the silhouette distinct at small sizes.

The symbol shares royal blue, sky blue, navy, and the idea of a folded wing with Yas Browser and Yas Mail. It does not reuse either product's falcon, envelope, or rounded blue app tile.

Royal blue `#2563EB`; sky blue `#86B9FF`; folded-base blue `#1747B1`; text navy `#152544`.

Wordmark: **yasapps.** in Dubai/Segoe UI. Font files are not redistributed. Use the symbol separately for favicons and social avatars. Leave at least one fifth of the mark's width clear around it. Do not stretch it, add flag stripes, or replace product icons with the parent identity inside the apps.
