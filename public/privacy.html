This policy covers the Yas Apps website and its product-update registration form. It does not describe every data flow inside the desktop applications; app-specific information is included with each preview.

What the form collects

We store your email address, selected products, preferred operating system, registration timestamps, and the version of the consent wording you accepted. We use this information for the Yas product and beta updates you requested.

Where it is stored

The website is hosted on Vercel. Registrations are stored in a Neon PostgreSQL database in Frankfurt, Germany. Hosting and database providers process this data to operate the site. We do not sell registration addresses.

Abuse protection

The registration service checks email-domain mail records and limits repeated submissions. For rate limiting, it stores a keyed hash derived from the request IP and time window, not the raw IP address, in the registration database. The hosting provider may separately process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the service.

Support payments

When enabled, voluntary support payments take place on Stripe’s hosted checkout. Stripe processes the payment and information you enter under its privacy policy. Yas Apps does not collect card details through this website or copy supporter emails into the release-news list. Our verified payment-event handler records only the event identifier, event type, payment state, and test/live status in hosting logs. Payment records remain in Stripe for the business to manage. Signing up for product emails is a separate choice.

No mailbox access

Registering on this site does not connect your inbox, request an email password, create a Yas mailbox, or create a login account. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website.

Your choices

Registration is optional. You can download available previews without registering. Re-registering the same address updates its product and platform preferences. Product emails should include an opt-out link. Until the mailing service is activated, registration only saves your interest; it does not send an automated confirmation email.

Retention and deletion

Registration details are kept until a removal request is processed or the product-update list is discontinued. Use the private form below to request deletion, opt out, or ask for access. The form records a request for the project owner to review; it does not immediately delete records or send an automated email. We may need to confirm ownership before disclosing or changing personal information. Privacy requests are retained only as needed to handle them. Please do not post your email address in a public GitHub issue.
